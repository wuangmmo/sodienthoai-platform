# Trust, Verification & Reputation V6

Trust is derived from explicit verification state, the strongest public identity confidence, data quality and spam risk. A disputed number is capped at a low trust score.

Claims can collect structured verification evidence without exposing evidence publicly. Evidence supports website, document, DNS, phone and other proof types and remains pending until moderation.

Spam score and trust score are separate signals: spam represents approved abuse reports while trust represents confidence in the number's public identity and data quality. SEO eligibility remains conservative and disputed records are routed to review.
